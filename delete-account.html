Delete Your SquadHub Account

Last updated: 28 October 2026

If you no longer want to use SquadHub, you can request that your account and the data associated with it be deleted. This page explains how to do that, and exactly what happens to your data when you do.

How to request deletion

  1. Email squadhubapp@gmail.com from the same email address you use to sign in to SquadHub.
  2. Use the subject line "Delete my account", and let us know the name of your team(s) in the app so we can locate the right records.
  3. We'll confirm your identity against the account email and process the deletion within 14 days, then send you confirmation once it's done.

If you coach more than one team under the same account, let us know in your email whether you want all teams deleted, or only specific ones.

What gets deleted

Once a deletion request is completed, the following are permanently removed from our systems:

  • Your account (login credentials, email address)
  • All player records for your team(s) — names, shirt numbers, positions, availability, and stats
  • Coach and team administrator records, including qualification data
  • Fixture, result, and match event history
  • Any photos you uploaded (player photos, team badge)

What's retained, and for how long

DataRetention after deletion
Account and team dataRemoved within 14 days of request
Backup copiesAutomatically purged from backups within 30 days
Anonymised usage logsMay be retained indefinitely, but contain no information that identifies you or your players

We don't retain any personal data beyond what's described above. If you've exported a backup file from within the app before deleting your account, that copy remains on your own device — deleting your account does not reach backups you've downloaded yourself.

Prefer to delete things yourself first?

You don't need to request full account deletion just to remove specific records. Within the app itself, you can at any time:

  • Delete individual players, coaches, or fixtures
  • Remove a photo from a player, coach, or team badge
  • Export a full backup before making any changes, so you always have your own copy
